I'm a first-year mechanical engineering PhD student at [UW-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/) advised by [Dr. Xiangru Xu](https://sites.google.com/site/xiangruxuhome/) in the [Xu Research Group](http://xu.me.wisc.edu). My research focuses on improving the performance and safety of autonomous systems in the face of uncertain dynamics. Combining stochastic methods with control theoretic guarantees provides a promising direction for control cyber-physical systems in dynamic environments.

While getting my undergrad degree in physics, I was fortunate to visit UW over summer 2019 for research in the [BADGER Lab](https://uwbadgerlab.engr.wisc.edu/) with Dr. Peter Adamczyk. It was then I discovered how awesome Madison is and now I can't imagine grad school anywhere else, but I'll re-assess after [January](https://www.usclimatedata.com/climate/madison/wisconsin/united-states/uswi0411). In spare time I enjoy flipping (and eating) crepes, building robots and practicing Judo. My CV can be viewed [here](CV.pdf).
