My research investigates Learning Based Model Predictive Control (LBMPC) for autonomous systems. I focus on using [Gaussian Processes](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/gaussian_process.html) (GPs) to learn system dynamics in a real-world environments.

The Gaussian Process – a stochastic method used in many STEM fields – is a data-driven, nonparametric approach for modeling functions with probabilistic guarantees. In the field of autonomy, GPs are revolutionary for their ability to efficiently learn models of real-world systems – systems with uncertain and partially-known dynamics.

GP-based control achieves better model accuracy than conventional Model Predictive Control (MPC) which utilizes a static *a priori* model and is more data and time efficient than Reinforcement Learning (RL) methods. The use of GPs to model autonomous systems is important for advancing probabilistic robotics, paving the way for autonomy of complex systems in extreme environments, and allowing for major improvements in performance, safety and robustness.
